🔧 Audio diagnostics

Speech speed is a request to your device's voice engine. Some installed voices ignore or clamp the playback rate. A difference cannot be guaranteed across iOS versions.

Speak Korean · 말해 봐요

Start with listening and imitation. Build towards answering Eomma without seeing the Korean sentence.

Record locally, listen back, then ask a parent to verify. Recording is not automatic pronunciation grading.

Recording requires HTTPS and microphone permission. This GitHub Pages app records in your browser only and does not upload audio. Recordings are temporary and disappear on reload or when replaced. iPhone Safari may not support browser-provided Korean speech recognition, even if recording works. Browser recognition may send speech to its service; it is optional, and a transcript is not a pronunciation score.
